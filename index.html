LAST LIGHT

Keep it alive.

A little flame is all that's left of the light. Guide it with one finger, catch fireflies to keep it burning, and slip past the shadows. How long can you keep it alive?

How to play

  • Drag anywhere on the screen. The flame follows your finger's movement, so your finger never hides it.
  • Fireflies restore the flame's energy. It slowly fades on its own.
  • Shadows drain the flame when they touch it. The light shrinks as the flame weakens.
  • Your score is how long the flame survives. Your best time is saved on your iPhone.

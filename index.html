LAST LIGHT

Relight the world.

The lanterns have gone out, and a little flame is all that's left of the light. Guide it through dark ruins, relight every lantern, and wake the great brazier at the end of each level. 24 levels in 3 chapters, three stars each.

How to play

  • Drag anywhere on the screen. The flame follows your finger's movement, so your finger never hides it.
  • Touch a lantern to light it. Its light refills the flame and keeps shadows out. Once every lantern burns, touch the great brazier to finish the level.
  • The flame's light fades in the dark and shrinks as it weakens. Fireflies restore it.
  • Shadows slow down and burn in bright light: a strong flame holds them back, a weak one cannot. Wardens patrol, dark wells breed shadows until the nearest lantern is lit, and stalkers resist light.
  • A lantern with a coloured stone opens the shadow gates of its colour.
  • Stars: light the brazier, beat the par time, finish untouched. Your progress is saved on your iPhone.

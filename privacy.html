LAST LIGHT

Privacy policy · Politique de confidentialité

In short

Last Light does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

What stays on your device

The game saves your progress (the stars and best time of each level, and the number of levels played) and your sound preference in a small file on your iPhone. This file never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party SDKs, advertising networks or analytics services.

Children

Because no data is collected from anyone, no data is collected from children.

Changes and contact

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published on this page. For any question, use the support page.
